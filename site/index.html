First step: your own domain

The initial business idea is simple: buy a domain name and top up hosting credits in one place. The key design choice is that you keep ownership. The domain lives in your own registrar account under your name; Suaka only automates the boring parts, like DNS setup, when you ask it to.

How the planned flow works: create your account, verify it, pick your domain, and approve exactly what Suaka may do on your behalf. Sign-up, payment, and approvals always stay with you.

This flow is a design, researched against the registrar's real API. No storefront, checkout, or account system exists yet. The domain automation guide documents the research and the planned customer wizard.

Principles

  • You own your domain. Registered under your details, in an account you control.
  • No lock-in. Your domain and DNS stay portable; approvals can be withdrawn.
  • Privacy by default. WHOIS privacy on; only the access you explicitly grant.
  • Transparent automation. Every delegated action is scoped, visible, and reversible.

Suaka is an early-stage concept. No products, domain sales, or hosting services are available yet, and nothing on this page is a working service or a price offer.
